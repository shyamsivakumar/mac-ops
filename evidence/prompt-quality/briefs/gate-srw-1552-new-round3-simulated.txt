## What happens to each open trade

- Capability off (default): behaviour is identical to `origin/main`. Open plans,
  pending attempts, client order ids and cumulative fills are read and written as
  before.
- Capability on, trade with no exit yet: the next stop, time or manual full exit
  sells at the positive fresh bid, reprices at most once, and falls back to MARKET
  after at most 45 seconds. A missing, zero or stale bid sends MARKET at once.
- Capability on, trade with a resting exit limit: the limit keeps its saved start
  time. Once the fallback is due, the limit is reconciled (cancel, then re-read
  fills) before one MARKET for the proven residual.
- Strict 1DTE deadline: a passed deadline sends MARKET without reading a quote.
- Unknown prior sell: reconciled by deterministic client order id before any
  replacement; a competing sell blocks that trade's submission only.

## Durable intent

next tick retries the same deterministic client order id

write failure for every exit, capability or not (SRW-1381), so the default path
is main's: `bot2/srw924_failed_save_exit.proof.cjs` requires that no order leaves
without a durable intent and proves restart recovery without a second sell.
